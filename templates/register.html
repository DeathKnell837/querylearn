Participant Information & Consent

You are invited to participate in a research case study investigating the relative readability and productivity benefits of Domain-Specific Languages (SQL) compared to General-Purpose Languages (procedural Python) among novice database learners.

• Anonymity: You will be assigned an anonymous Study ID. No personal identification (names, student numbers) will be stored or published.

• Activities: You will complete short code interpretation questions, 6 programming tasks in two language conditions (8 minutes each maximum), and a post-task feedback questionnaire.

• Voluntary: Your participation is completely voluntary and will not affect your academic grades. You may withdraw at any time without penalty.
